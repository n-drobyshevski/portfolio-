About

Hi, I'm Nick. I design and build websites and web apps for shops, cafés, trades and anyone with a good idea. You deal with me from the first chat to launch day, and I'm still around after.

Why hire one person instead of an agency?

  1. 1

    You always know who is doing the work, because it's me. No account manager passing messages along, no junior learning on your project.

  2. 2

    The price we agree on before I start is the price you pay. If something takes me longer than planned, that's my problem.

  3. 3

    When it's done, it all belongs to you: the design files, the code, the domain and the hosting account. You can leave whenever you want and take everything with you.
